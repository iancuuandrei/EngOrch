## Limits

This small paired run does not qualify isolated writers: one of two isolated
tasks remained blocked after its repair budget, and the final candidate did
not reach READY. It also does not establish a speedup or general performance
effect; the sample is two tasks, only one isolated task completed, and elapsed
times are task durations rather than a controlled end-to-end throughput
comparison. The earlier v1.0.7 failed arms remain historical evidence and were
not resumed or rewritten by this run.
